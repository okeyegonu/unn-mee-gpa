## Ambiguities in the source documents

These are reported rather than guessed at, as required by the specification.

1. **`PHY 104` and `STA 112` (First Year, Second Semester) have no unit load on the form.** Both appear in
   the handwritten revision, but no number is written against them in the unit column, and no total is
   written for the revised second semester either, so the values could not be recovered by arithmetic.
   **Resolved on 2026-09-17:** the department supplied `PHY 104` = 2 units and `STA 112` = 3 units. Both
   are marked `"units_source": "supplied_by_department"` in `data/curriculum.json` and recorded under
   `amendments`, so it stays clear that these two figures were provided rather than extracted. With them
   in place the revised second semester totals 16 units, matching the revised first semester.
2. **Most revised First Year course titles are not stated.** The form lists codes only. Where a new code
   plainly replaces a struck-out printed course (for example `MTH 101` over `MTH 111`), that course’s
   printed title has been carried across and marked `"title_source": "inherited_from_replaced_course"`.
   Where there is no such predecessor — `PHY 103`, `GET 101` and the whole of the revised second
   semester — the title is left `null` rather than invented.
3. **The First Year and the later years come from different revisions of the programme.** The handwritten
   form uses the newer numbering (`MTH 101`, `GST 111`, `MEE 101`, `GET 102`); the 2023 bachelors
   document still prints the older numbering for First Year (`MTH 111`, `GSP 101`, `EGR 101`) and uses
   `MEE/EGR/CVE/EEE/GSP` codes throughout Years 2–5. Years 2–5 are reproduced exactly as printed.
4. **First Year, Second Semester: printed list versus handwritten list.** The printed list
   (`MTH 122`, `CHM 112`, `CHM 122`, `PHY 116`, `PHY 124`, `GSP 102`, `ENGR 102`) is not struck out on
   the form, but a complete parallel list in the revised numbering is written beside it. The handwritten
   list is treated as the current one; the printed list is retained in the data file under
   `superseded_printed_list` so the decision can be reviewed and reversed.
5. **`GSP 111` (The Use of Library and Study Skills, 2 units) is struck out with no replacement.** It is
   recorded under `removed_from_earlier_revision` and does not appear in the calculator.
6. **Fifth Year electives are option areas, not a free pool.** Option A (Thermal and Fluids) and Option B
   (Design, Materials and Manufacturing) are alternatives: a student chooses two courses (6 units) in the
   first semester and one course (3 units) in the second from *one* option area. Both option areas are
   listed in full and the courses are marked as electives; the calculator counts whichever electives the
   student actually enters a grade for, and does not enforce the choice rule.

## How to correct this data

Edit `data/curriculum.json` only — the GPA engine, the storage layer and the interface read it at
runtime and contain no course definitions. Then run `npm run validate` to regenerate this report and
`data/curriculum.flat.json`. If the change is substantive, raise `curriculum_version`; results saved
under the previous version are kept separately and are never overwritten.
